  • Gourmet catering throughout the day

  • iGaming executives (all skill levels welcome)

The most powerful iGaming conversations don't happen at booths or on stages. They happen around a table, late in the evening, with people you trust. What's included
  • Private vineyard estate for 2 days
  • Wine tasting & cellar tour with sommelier
  • Three curated dinners with industry themes
  • Morning sessions: insight sharing & trend mapping
  • Personal concierge & luxury accommodation
  • Game Changers Club gifts & materials

  • 18 senior iGaming leaders maximum
